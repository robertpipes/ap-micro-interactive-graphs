Government-Imposed Tax & Tax Burden

Change the tax and demand elasticity. Watch the tax burden shift between buyers and sellers.
Increase the tax to shift Supply upward.
Both demand curves start at the same original equilibrium so students can compare the tax incidence.
Key Rule
The more inelastic side of the market bears the greater share of the tax burden.
